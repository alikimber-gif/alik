# NCC Social Media

## For future Claude

NCC Social Media is the social media management project for Nelson City Council, owned by [[people/Ali Kimber]]. Status: active as of 2026-07-12. Covers content strategy, scheduling, analytics, and community engagement across council social channels.

## Overview

Social media presence for Nelson City Council. Manages content across Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and other platforms. Focus on community engagement, council updates, events, and public information.

## Channels

- Facebook - primary community engagement channel
- Instagram - visual storytelling, events, local highlights
- LinkedIn - council updates, professional engagement, recruitment

## Content categories

- Council updates and decisions
- Community events and activities
- Public safety and infrastructure
- Environmental and sustainability initiatives
- Local stories and community highlights
